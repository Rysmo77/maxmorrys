Rysmo puts your Max-Morrys training in your hand.

The app opens the courses you are enrolled in on maxmorrys.me. It sells nothing: what you own, you open here.

WHAT IT DOES

- Your courses, module by module, lesson by lesson. Progress is counted on the server, not guessed.
- A tutor that knows your syllabus. You ask it the question you ask no one else; it answers in the context of what you are learning.
- Your notes, next to the lesson that prompted them.
- Your certificates, with the verification code an employer can open without an account.
- The Digitos Club, if you are a member: the feed, discussions, the calendar, opportunities.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO, AND WHY THAT IS WRITTEN DOWN

No purchases are possible inside the app. No advertising. No tracking — not a single third-party measurement tool. And no figure is shown without the date it was read: when we do not know, the screen writes "not recorded" rather than zero.

BUILT FOR A METERED DATA PLAN

What you download, you asked for. Nothing loads "to be helpful": that would spend your credit without telling you.

YOUR ACCOUNT

The same one as on maxmorrys.me. You can export your data and delete your account from inside the app, without going through the website.
